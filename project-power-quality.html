LV network power-quality analysis

Electrical measurement analysis covering phase loading, power factor and network quality.

Discipline

Electrical

Technology

C.A 8334 · 230/400 V · 90 A

Focus

Technical analysis

Network-analysis report — measurement context and engineering scope.

Project objective

Analyse the condition of a low-voltage network from Chauvin Arnoux C.A 8334 measurements recorded between 13 and 16 October 2026.

Engineering work

  • Analysed voltage, current, active/reactive/apparent power and power factor.
  • Reviewed flicker and harmonic measurements.
  • Prepared the analysis report and prioritised technical recommendations.

Deliverable

A signed October 2026 report identifying phase-loading and power-factor issues, with recommended corrective actions.
